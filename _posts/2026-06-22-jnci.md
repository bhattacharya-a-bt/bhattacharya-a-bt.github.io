Our collaborative paper with the Sieh Lab and Kaiser Permanente, [*Comparative 10-year performance of mammography artificial intelligence, polygenic, and clinical breast cancer risk models in the Kaiser Permanente Research Bank*](https://doi.org/10.1093/jnci/djag154),
is published in the *Journal of the National Cancer Institute*. Congrats to the team, including Taylor!
