Our NIEHS R21 (ES039048), *"Identifying PFAS-Induced Disruption of Placental Imprinting and Neurodevelopment Through Multi-Omic Negative Control Calibration,"* has been funded!
This project builds on our placental genomics work to understand how PFAS exposures disrupt placental imprinting and downstream neurodevelopment.
