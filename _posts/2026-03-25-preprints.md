Two new preprints from the lab are out on bioRxiv:

- Sean's work with the Huang Lab showing that [natural variation in transplacental transfer efficiency exposes distinct transcriptional network architectures of PFAS effects](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.23.712893) on birth weight and gestational age.
- Taylor's work [improving isoform-level eQTL and integrative genetic analyses of breast cancer risk with long-read RNA transcript assemblies](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.22.713514), with Aryun, Yung-Han, and Sean.
