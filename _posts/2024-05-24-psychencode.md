Two collaborative PsychENCODE papers are out! The [cross-ancestry atlas of gene, isoform, and splicing regulation in the developing human brain](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adh0829)
is published in *Science*, and our work on [brain cell-type shifts in Alzheimer's disease, autism, and schizophrenia](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn7655)
is published in *Science Advances*. Congratulations to the Gandal Lab and all our collaborators!
