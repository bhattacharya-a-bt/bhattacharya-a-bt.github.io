Our NCI R21 (CA293419), *"Alternative splicing and isoform expression as mediators for the genetic etiology of breast cancer,"* has been funded!
This project will use long- and short-read RNA-seq to understand how isoform-level regulation links germline genetics to breast cancer risk.
