New R21 funding to study PFAS and placental imprinting

Arjun Bhattacharya, PhD Principal Investigator
September 09, 2026 October 28, 2026
news funding

Our NIEHS R21 (ES039048), “Identifying PFAS-Induced Disruption of Placental Imprinting and Neurodevelopment Through Multi-Omic Negative Control Calibration,” has been funded! This project builds on our placental genomics work to understand how PFAS exposures disrupt placental imprinting and downstream neurodevelopment.
